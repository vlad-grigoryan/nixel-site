Why not charge for the AI?

Because the model is what finds names, addresses and organisations, and that is protection. Charging for it would mean free users are less covered, in a product whose only job is to cover you. It also happens to be the least predictable part of the product, and selling the least predictable part is a poor way to earn trust.
