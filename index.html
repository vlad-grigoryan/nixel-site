Nixel

Hide what shouldn't be shared — before you share it.

Nixel finds API keys, passwords, card numbers, emails and personal details on any page and covers them up. Then you screen-share, screenshot or record without thinking about it.

Add to Chrome

Free. No account. Nothing to configure.

Hello Vlad Morozov, your statement is attached. A copy went to v.morozov@fastmail.com and we will call +7 916 402 55 18 if anything needs your attention.

Press any mask to look under it. The original never leaves the page.

Everything happens on your device

This is the part that matters. Nixel does not send the pages you visit anywhere, because there is nowhere to send them — there is no server behind this product. Detection runs inside your browser, in your own machine's memory.

That is not a promise about how carefully we handle your data. It is a statement that we never receive it.

What it finds

  • API keys, access tokens and AWS credentials
  • Card numbers, verified against a checksum and an issuer prefix
  • Email addresses and phone numbers
  • Internal hostnames and private IP addresses
  • People's names, addresses and organisations — using a language model that also runs on your device

The popup tells you what it found, and one tap stops it hiding any kind you don't care about.

Also does screenshots

Capture the visible page and Nixel opens an editor with every finding already covered. Toggle any of them, pick how a mask looks, then copy or save a PNG that is safe to send.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Nixel is a browser extension published by SD Software LLC (Armenia). This policy describes exactly what the extension does with data. It is short because the extension does very little.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and receive no data from the extension. We cannot see the pages you visit, what Nixel found on them, or that you use it at all. There is no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting.

What stays on your device

  • Page content. Read in your browser to find sensitive text, and never transmitted. It is not written to disk or kept after the page closes.
  • Your settings. Which kinds to hide, how masks look, which sites are switched off. Stored locally by your browser.
  • Screenshots. A capture is held in your browser's temporary storage only while the editor is open, and is deleted when you close it.

Network requests the extension makes

Two, and only these:

Downloading the detection model — once The optional on-device language model (about 100 MB) is downloaded from the Hugging Face CDN the first time you enable it, then cached and used offline. This request contains no information about you or the pages you visit. You can decline it, remove the model at any time, and the rest of the extension keeps working.
Checking a Pro licence — if you buy one Paid users' extensions periodically confirm a licence key with our payment provider. The request contains the licence key and a device label. It never contains page content, and free users' extensions never make it.

Permissions, and why

  • activeTab / scripting — to read and mask the page you are looking at.
  • storage — to remember your settings.
  • offscreen — to run the detection model without slowing the page down.

Payments

If you buy Pro, the transaction is handled by our payment provider, who acts as merchant of record and collects the billing details needed to process it under their own privacy policy. We never see or store your card details.

Children

Nixel is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions, or a request about your data: support@nixel.org.
