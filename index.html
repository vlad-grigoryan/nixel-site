Before you install

A few useful details.

Does it work with Zoom, Google Meet or Loom?

Nixel masks text in Chrome pages. Those masks can appear when you share or record that browser tab with your meeting or recording tool. Nixel doesn't mask other apps, browser controls or your whole desktop. Check the shared view before starting.

Do I need to download the language model?

No. Pattern-based detection covers supported credentials, card numbers, emails, phone numbers and network details without it. Enable the optional local model if you also want to detect names, addresses and organisations.

Will it catch everything?

Detection can miss sensitive details or hide text you wanted to show. Nixel works with supported text on web pages; don't rely on it to find text inside images or videos. Review your page and any exported screenshot before sharing.
