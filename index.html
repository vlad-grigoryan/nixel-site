Everything happens on your device

Nixel does not send the pages you visit anywhere, because there is nowhere to send them. There is no server behind this product. Detection runs inside your browser, in your own machine's memory.

That is not a promise about how carefully we handle your data. It is a statement that we never receive it.

What it finds

  • API keys, access tokens and AWS credentials
  • Card numbers, verified against a checksum and an issuer prefix
  • Email addresses and phone numbers
  • Internal hostnames and private IP addresses
  • People's names, addresses and organisations, found by a language model that also runs on your device

The popup tells you what it found, and one tap stops it hiding any kind you don't care about.

The screenshot you were about to send

Right now you crop it, or you smudge it in Preview, or you look at it twice and send it anyway. All three depend on you spotting the thing you are trying not to send.

Nixel captures the page and opens an editor with every finding already covered. You review what it caught rather than hunt for what you missed. Toggle any of them, pick how a mask looks, then copy or save a PNG that is safe to paste into Slack, a ticket or a reply.
